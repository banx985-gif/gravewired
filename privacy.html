Gravewired privacy policy

Banx Games · last updated 14 November 2026

Gravewired is a game. We collect only what the game needs to work, keep your progress safe and stop cheating. We don't sell your information.

What is kept on your phone

Your save (progress, items, settings) is kept on your phone or in your browser. A crash log is kept on your phone too, and leaves it only if you copy it yourself (Settings → Help).

What goes to our server (when online play is on)

  • A guest account: a random account number, made the first time you play online. No name or email is needed.
  • Your game progress: levels, items, currencies, event and Tournament results, and a record of each change, so a dropped connection can never pay twice and nobody can cheat their way up.
  • Your email, only if you add it (Settings → Online account), so you can keep your progress and sign in on another phone. We use it only to send you a sign-in code or confirmation link.
  • A random device number, so one run can't be played on two phones at once.
  • Purchase records: the store's order number and what was bought, so a purchase is delivered once and can be restored. Payment details stay with Google Play; we never see them.

The server is run by Supabase for us. Like any website, it sees your internet address while it answers; we don't store it with your account.

Ads

Ads are optional: you choose to watch one for a reward. When real ads are switched on in the app, Google AdMob shows them and may use your phone's advertising ID; you can reset or turn that off in your phone's settings. Players in Europe and the UK are asked for consent first.

Notifications

The game can remind you about rewards and events. These are scheduled on your phone; you can turn each kind off in Settings.

Deleting your account

Settings → Online account → Delete my account removes your account and all its progress from our server and from that phone. It can't be undone.

Children

Gravewired isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above changes too. Questions: use the contact email on Gravewired's Google Play page.
